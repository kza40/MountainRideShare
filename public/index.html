About Us

We started Mountain Ride Share with a simple goal: to take some of the load off our local transportation system and give back to the environment. By sharing rides, we’re making commutes easier, helping people connect, and reducing our collective carbon footprint. Join us on this journey towards a cleaner, greener way to travel!
